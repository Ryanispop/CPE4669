Scalability Discussion

Performance improved as the rank count increased from 1 to 64. The best result was 0.222428 seconds with 64 ranks, giving a speedup of 8.776. Increasing to 128 ranks did not improve performance, and efficiency fell from 13.7% at 64 ranks to 6.8% at 128 ranks. This indicates that communication, merging, and memory overhead outweighed the benefit of dividing the local sorting work among more ranks.
